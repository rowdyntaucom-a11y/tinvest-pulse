# Analytics display ownership

To prevent duplicated widgets across QVANIX sections:

- Portfolio owns capital, cash result, account metadata, positions and asset-class allocation.
- Analytics owns performance, benchmark and risk/health metrics.
- Income owns dividends/coupons and later payout calendar, YoC and payout-growth metrics.
- DNA consumes analytics/gamification state but does not duplicate financial dashboards.

A metric may be referenced contextually in another module, but its full widget and methodology belong to exactly one primary section.
