# QVANIX — Branding decision

Approved public product name: **QVANIX** / **Кваникс**.

Decision date: 2026-09-11.

## Rules
- Public UI, future app-store listing, landing pages and share cards should use `QVANIX` rather than `TInvest Pulse` / `Пульс`.
- `Кряхтящий фонд` remains the current personal portfolio/account name and may appear as user data, not as the public product brand.
- Internal repository, Render service names and legacy `/v2` URLs may keep historical `tinvest-pulse` identifiers until a controlled infrastructure migration; do not rename them ad hoc and risk breaking deployments.
- DNA branding may use `QVANIX DNA` as the living-world module name.
- Full AI chat remains deferred until monetization; QVANIX must first be valuable through deterministic analytics and the living-world system.
- Before a public commercial launch, perform formal trademark/domain/store clearance again, including similar-sounding marks. Preliminary exact-name web searches on 2026-09-11 did not surface an indexed exact `QVANIX` company/app/trademark result, but this is not a legal clearance.

## Product positioning
QVANIX is an explainable investment intelligence platform with a living portfolio world. The brand should feel technical and modern without being tied to one broker, one country, or one feature.
