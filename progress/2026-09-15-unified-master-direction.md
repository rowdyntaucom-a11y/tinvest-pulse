# QVANIX unified master direction — 2026-09-15

## Why this checkpoint exists

The project received four new planning inputs covering:
- positioning/growth relative to Snowball Income;
- technical scale roadmap;
- UX copy/glossary/design brief;
- UX/navigation redesign guidance.

These were reconciled against the actual current QVANIX state rather than copied literally.

## Durable decisions

1. Snowball remains a benchmark for usability, onboarding, distribution and scale, but QVANIX is not a Snowball clone and should not center its brand on the competitor.
2. QVANIX positioning: deeper, transparent portfolio intelligence for investors who have outgrown a basic tracker.
3. The old universal “everything must fit in one viewport” rule is retired. Canonical rule: `FIRST SCREEN MUST ANSWER. DEPTH MAY SCROLL.`
4. Single-screen presentation survives as dedicated Pulse/Screenshot mode.
5. Product work is organized into three tracks: Product Depth, UX/Comprehension, Scale/Growth.
6. UX/Comprehension is the immediate next major Epic before large multi-user/acquisition infrastructure.
7. Scale roadmap is corrected for actual implementation: T-Invest data access already exists; the future gap is secure per-user multi-user broker connection architecture, not writing T-Invest integration from scratch.
8. Multi-user foundation precedes multi-portfolio/payment/store scale work.
9. Primary mobile navigation should move away from clipping/wrapping pill rows; deep secondary navigation should use progressive disclosure/section selectors rather than exposing several permanent rows.
10. DNA remains first-class and must not disappear merely to force a five-item navigation pattern.
11. Pult should establish a strong hero hierarchy but must not invent a daily-change metric. Daily Movers/daily return remain gated until a verified previous-close/daily-change contract exists.
12. Existing `ContextHelp` should be reused/extended for glossary/help rather than creating a competing tooltip system.
13. Dark QVANIX identity remains primary; improve contrast/hierarchy first. A light shell may be researched later as an optional theme.
14. Marketing/SEO/competitive claims must not use unverified competitor statistics as established facts.
15. Full AI chat remains frozen for MVP; deterministic analytics remain the product core.
