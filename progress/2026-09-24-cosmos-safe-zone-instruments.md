# 2026-09-24 — Cosmos safe-zone instrument pass

Evidence: Samsung recording `1000031544.mp4`.

## Finding
The new functional widgets were materially better than equal placeholder cards, but the 2x2 decks still behaved like a dark blanket across the lower half of Mix C. On Assets, Analysis and Income the main figure was repeatedly crossed by card surfaces. Goal still had an oversized title/target stack and repeated target information.

## Correction
- Home remains unchanged.
- Assets, Analysis and Income now use left/right HUD rails instead of a full-width 2x2 deck.
- A central focal corridor is intentionally left open for the main figure and world art.
- Widget material is lighter and more transparent; borders/geometry carry hierarchy rather than dark panel mass.
- Each tab retains its distinct instruments:
  - Assets: allocation, holdings, concentration, asset intelligence.
  - Analysis: performance, risk, IMOEX, correlation.
  - Income: cash flow, calendar, sources, coverage.
- Goal title is reduced to a compact chapter marker.
- Goal target editor becomes a small right-side command surface.
- The duplicate target instrument is removed visually; capital, route and scenarios frame the art from the edges.
- No financial values are fabricated and no functionality is removed.

## Mobile rule
Artwork is not decoration behind a dashboard. Cosmos internal tabs should read as instrumentation embedded into the world. The focal subject must stay readable on real Samsung/Android footage.
