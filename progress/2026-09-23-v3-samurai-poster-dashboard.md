# 2026-09-23 — Samurai poster-dashboard structural reset

Real Samsung evidence after #587 showed that incremental density fixes had converged on the same composition. This pass deliberately changes the mobile hierarchy rather than tuning it again: the approved Ronin art becomes one clean poster stage; portfolio answer is anchored at its lower edge; status controls become small scene instruments; all four primary metrics move into a compact 2×2 command dock below the poster; history and deeper sections begin on a separate opaque data plane. This removes the half-art/half-card overlap visible on device and preserves the first-screen answer without turning the artwork into wallpaper.

Scope is Samurai Home mobile presentation only. Financial semantics, API/Data Trust, DNA, other shells and generated artwork are unchanged. No additional animation/network cost was added.
