# QVANIX resume checkpoint — 2026-09-25

## Current product direction
- Snowball is the minimum functional-breadth benchmark for QVANIX, not a UI template.
- Implement full product depth in **one shell first: Samurai**.
- Only after Samurai reaches the agreed Snowball+ breadth do we transfer the shared finance/workflow architecture into Cosmos, NORD and later shells.
- Each shell keeps its own composition, materials, motion and atmosphere; finance engines/data contracts stay shared.
- Ordinary tabs may extend vertically through as many chapters as needed. First viewport answers quickly; depth may scroll. Pulse/Screenshot mode remains the one-screen exception.
- DNA WORLD remains frozen / last priority until the core investment product is mature.

- risk, concentration, diversification and report views;
- bond analytics;
- goals and scenario comparison;
- rebalancing;
- portfolio laboratory / historical strategy comparison;
- technical discovery such as "fallen assets";
