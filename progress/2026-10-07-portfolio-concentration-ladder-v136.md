# QVANIX v136 — Portfolio Concentration Ladder

- Complements HHI/Top-N concentration with an intuitive cumulative-capital ladder.
- Reports minimum number of current positive-value positions required to cross 25%, 50%, 75% and 90% of portfolio capital.
- Shows actual reached share because discrete positions can overshoot each threshold.
- Compares capital held by the largest half of positions versus the remaining half.
- Zero, negative and non-finite position values are excluded rather than treated as capital.
- Descriptive current structure only; no optimal allocation claim or recommendation.
