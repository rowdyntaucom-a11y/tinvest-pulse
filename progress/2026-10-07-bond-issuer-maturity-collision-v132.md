# QVANIX v132 — Bond Issuer × Maturity Collision

- Adds the missing intersection between issuer concentration and maturity-year concentration.
- Uses only positive current bond capital with confirmed non-perpetual maturity dates.
- Shows largest issuer-year cell as share of all dated bond capital and share inside its maturity year.
- Shows each maturity year's capital share, dominant issuer share, issuer count and issue count.
- Perpetual/undated positions are deliberately excluded rather than assigned synthetic maturity.
- Descriptive concentration only; not credit risk, default probability or recommendation.
